In late October, Shanghai Film Technology Factory (上海电影技术厂) and Hippie Screening Studio present **Echoes of Times**, a programme of 4K restored classics: *Crows and Sparrows* by Zheng Junli, starring Shangguan Yunzhu, and two films by Xie Jin, *Big Li, Little Li and Old Li* and *The Legend of Tianyun Mountain*. Three films from three eras, from the eve of 1949 to the early years of reform, each an echo of its time.

**The Films**

**Crows and Sparrows 乌鸦与麻雀**

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/blog/echoes_of_times/poster/crows_and_sparrows.jpg" alt="Crows and Sparrows 乌鸦与麻雀" />
  <figcaption>Crows and Sparrows 乌鸦与麻雀</figcaption>
</figure>

Shanghai, winter 1948. The tenants of a shared lane house suddenly face eviction: Hou Yibo, a corrupt Nationalist official planning to flee with his loot, wants to sell the building he seized. As the regime collapses around them, the tenants, a proofreader, street vendors, a schoolteacher and their families, each scheme for themselves, until the threat pushes them toward solidarity. Zheng Junli turns one small building into a portrait of a whole society at the end of an era, with sharp satire and a vivid ensemble cast. Shot at Kunlun Studios under Nationalist censorship on the eve of the Communist victory, it is a landmark of Chinese realist cinema.

**Big Li, Little Li and Old Li 大李小李和老李**

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/blog/echoes_of_times/poster/big_little_old_li.jpg" alt="Big Li, Little Li and Old Li 大李小李和老李" />
  <figcaption>Big Li, Little Li and Old Li 大李小李和老李</figcaption>
</figure>

Shanghai in the 1960s. A meat processing plant is swept up in a nationwide sports craze, and the conservative workshop head Old Li wants nothing to do with it. Big Li, the union chairman who never liked exercise, ends up chairing the factory sports association; together with the sports-mad Little Li he wins over his colleagues through a string of hilarious misunderstandings and clashes. Xie Jin's only comedy, told with a light touch and a strong Shanghai local flavour, is a rare glimpse of ordinary working life and spirit in the early years of socialist construction.

**The Legend of Tianyun Mountain 天云山传奇**

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/blog/echoes_of_times/poster/legend_of_tianyun_mountain.jpg" alt="The Legend of Tianyun Mountain 天云山传奇" />
  <figcaption>The Legend of Tianyun Mountain 天云山传奇</figcaption>
</figure>

1978: an unredressed case resurfaces and takes us back twenty years to Tianyun Mountain. In the early 1950s, two young graduates, Song Wei and Feng Qinglan, join a mountain exploration team. When the Anti-Rightist Campaign begins in 1957, the political commissar Luo Qun, whom Song loves, is labelled a rightist. Song, under pressure, breaks with him; Feng chooses to stay by his side through long years of hardship. Interweaving personal fates with historical trauma, Xie Jin reflects on how political campaigns crush people. One of the first films to depict the Anti-Rightist Campaign, it won Best Picture and Best Director at the first Golden Rooster Awards (1981) and is a key work of early post-Mao reflection on history.
