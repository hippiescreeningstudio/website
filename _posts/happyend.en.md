This September, we screen _Happyend_, the first narrative feature by Neo Sora.

**Synopsis**

A near-future city in Japan awaits destruction as it is rocked by a series of foreshocks that predict a larger, more disastrous quake on the horizon. With the anxiety looming over them, a group of teenage best friends and musicians get into typical teenager trouble that tests the strength of their relationships.

The trouble in question is a prank on the school principal — and his answer to it is an AI surveillance system that scores every student in the building. Yuta shrugs it off and keeps chasing the next party; Kou, a Zainichi Korean, finds that the cameras look at him differently than they look at his friend. What begins as a last summer of music, motorbikes and nothing much to do slowly becomes a question neither of them expected to face so early: what do you do when the world you are about to inherit is already watching you?

**About the Director**

**Neo Sora 空音央** (b. 1991, New York) grew up between New York and Tokyo and studied philosophy and film. He made the short films _The Chicken_ (2020) and _Sugar Glass Bottle_ (2022), and the concert documentary _Ryuichi Sakamoto | Opus_ (2023), filmed with his father in what would be Sakamoto's final performance. _Happyend_ is his first narrative feature, and won him the Directors Guild of Japan New Directors Award.

**Awards**

Premiered in the Orizzonti section of the 81st Venice Film Festival in 2024, then travelled to Toronto and to the New York Film Festival. It went on to win the Young Cinema Award at the Asia Pacific Screen Awards, the Golden Hanoman at Jogja-NETPAC, the Roberto Rossellini Jury Award at Pingyao, and Best Screenplay at QCinema.
