Shadows and Echoes threads fourteen short films through pandemic aftershocks, the AI era, and the fantasies that surface in the cracks of everyday life. The programme breaks lived experience into multiple facets: solitude and connection in modern society, fiction as the most precise metaphor, and the borders between technology and intimacy. The films don't offer a unified answer — they trace what becomes possible after each collision.

**Q&A Guests**

Yueqi Wu and Kyrylo Alferiev, two of the three directors of *2024: A Swiss Odyssey*, will join the audience after the screening for a roughly 30-minute conversation. The film is co-directed by Yueqi Wu, Kyrylo Alferiev, and Florian Rudolph.

**About FIRST International Film Festival**

Established in 2006, the FIRST International Film Festival (FIRST IFF) is dedicated to discovering and supporting emerging filmmakers and their debut works. With a strong commitment to artistic innovation and public engagement, FIRST IFF continues to bring fresh vitality to Sinophone cinema and foster the sustainable development of the film ecosystem.

**About FIRST Public Programming**

"Films are meant to be watched." FIRST Public Programming is the non-profit screening initiative of the FIRST International Film Festival, dedicated to presenting works by emerging filmmakers beyond the mainstream gaze. Through public screenings and film-related events, it seeks to connect with new audiences, revive cinephile culture, and create spaces for diverse cinematic dialogue.
