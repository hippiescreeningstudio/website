**Synopsis**

When Meng Kerou helps her best friend pursue a boy from the school swim team, an unexpected emotional triangle begins to take shape. Set against sunlit streets, swimming pools, and long summer afternoons, Blue Gate Crossing captures the uncertainty, longing, and quiet discoveries of youth with remarkable tenderness.

More than twenty years after its release, the film remains a touching exploration of identity, affection, and the courage to understand one’s own feelings.
