In Northeast China, the city of Shenyang is slowly decaying. Xu Lingling, a 25-year-old woman suffering from uremia, finds her life falling apart. At the height of her illness, her estranged father returns, accompanied by her best friend Tan Na and former classmate Zhao Dongyang, to care for her. Though her days are filled with pain, as she faces the tangled emotions of family, love, and friendship, Lingling begins to realize that life is still worth living. One day, she decides to take a trip—with Tan Na and Zhao Dongyang by her side, she sets out on the open road.

The film is adapted from the novel of the same name by BAN Yu.

**About the Director**

<figure>

</figure>

_Liang Ming_ is a film director, screenwriter, and actor. A graduate of the Directing and Acting Department at the Communication University of China, Liang began his career in performance and assistant directing, participating in films such as Spring Fever (2009), Shadow Days (2014), and Ripples of Life (2021).

His directorial debut, Wisdom Tooth (2020), is noted for its subtle emotional expression and distinctive narrative style. The film won the Fei Mu Award for Best Director and the Roberto Rossellini Jury Award at the 3rd Pingyao International Film Festival, Best Director in the Firebird Award Competition at the 44th Hong Kong International Film Festival, and the Best New Director Award from the 29th Shanghai Film Critics Association. Liang was also nominated for Best New Director at the 14th Asian Film Awards.

Rooted in the landscapes and emotional textures of his native Northeast China, Liang’s works are driven more by human emotion than by plot, focusing on the delicate shifts within relationships and the interplay between inner feeling and environment. As Liang himself remarks, “There is nothing new under the sun; what is new is the way each of us feels.” This sensibility continues to define the poetic realism and emotional depth of his cinematic vision.
